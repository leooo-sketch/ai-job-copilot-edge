## PDF.js

The extension bundles minified browser files from [Mozilla PDF.js](https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js) (`pdfjs-dist` 4.8.69) to extract text from PDF resumes locally.

PDF.js is licensed under the Apache License 2.0. The full license is included at [`vendor/LICENSE.pdfjs.txt`](vendor/LICENSE.pdfjs.txt).

## Mammoth.js

The extension bundles [Mammoth.js](https://github.com/mwilliamson/mammoth.js) 1.12.2 to extract raw text from DOCX resumes locally.
