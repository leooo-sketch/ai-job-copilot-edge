# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good intent, protect user privacy, and discuss technical decisions rather than attacking people.

Examples of welcome behavior include giving actionable feedback, crediting others, accepting corrections, and redacting personal or account data from reports.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, credential sharing, spam, and helping others bypass platform safety or access controls.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this standard. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical.
